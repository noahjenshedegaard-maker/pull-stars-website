How to open it

The tear strip sits 12 mm above the cards, and the stiff ad card at the back keeps the stack flat.

  1. Hold it upright

    Front facing you, yellow strip at the top.

  2. Grab the tab

    It sticks out on the right, with a notch so you don't need a fingernail.

  3. Pull across

    The strip rips in one straight line and never touches the cards.

  4. Slide them out

    Pinch the edges and lift. Nothing bends.
